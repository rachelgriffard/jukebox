Start a jukebox on the screen with the speakers. Friends scan a code, add YouTube songs from their phones, and vote on what plays next.

Hosting

Open this on the laptop or TV that will play the music.

Joining

How the queue works

  1. Adding a song counts as your upvote.
  2. Highest score plays next. Ties go to whoever added first.
  3. A song that drops to −3 is removed.
  4. Three guests voting to skip ends the current song early.
